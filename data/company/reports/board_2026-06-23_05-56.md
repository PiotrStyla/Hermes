## Quality Director
Three recurring themes: (1) operators not following up on emotional cues and skipping required health/safety checks, (2) calls consistently running too long with rushed endings, (3) repetitive conversational loops. These hurt info_quality most severely (score dropped 0.7 points) and brevity (dropped 0.8 points). The trend shows declines across all axes. The core issue is a rigid script structure that forces operators to check boxes but lacks adaptive branching for emotional follow-ups, causing them to either miss key info or cram everything into an ever-lengthening call. The systemic fix: replace the linear script with a dynamic, adaptive dialog tree that automatically surfaces required health/safety questions when emotional follow-ups occur, keeps replies concise, and ends calls after a maximum of 12 turns. This gives the operator—and owner—a progressive, non-repetitive flow.
