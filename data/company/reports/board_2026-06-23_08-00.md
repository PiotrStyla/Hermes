## Quality Director
Three recurring themes dominate the recent issues. First, calls consistently run too long, causing rushed endings and user fatigue — this is crushing our brevity score, which dropped from 6.9 to 6.1. Second, operators routinely skip required health, medication, and safety checks, directly dragging down info_quality (7.8 → 7.1). Third, emotional follow-ups are missed and questions are fired too quickly, hurting both listening and warmth, which have also slipped. The owner rightly flags that conversations circle without progress.

A systemic fix: design a dynamic call script that front-loads all mandatory health/safety questions into the first few turns, then uses the senior’s responses to branch into shorter or longer emotional check-ins. Add an internal timer that gently prompts wrap-up after 12 turns. This ensures every call hits critical info targets, keeps length predictable, and frees the operator to genuinely listen — breaking the repetitive loop without micromanaging.
