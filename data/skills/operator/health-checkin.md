# Health Check-In

## Purpose
Conduct a routine health check-in with elderly callers to assess their well-being and identify any changes or concerns.

## Process

### Step 1: Greeting and Opening
Start with a warm and respectful greeting.
- Example: "Dzień dobry, tu [nazwa centrum]. Dzwonię, aby sprawdzić, jak się Pan/Pani dzisiaj czuje."

### Step 2: Mood Check-In
Ask about the caller's current mood or general feeling.
- Example: "Jak się Pan/Pani dzisiaj czuje? Czy wszystko w porządku?"
- Listen carefully and note any emotional or physical cues. If the caller expresses strong emotions or raises unrelated topics (e.g., family, financial matters), first show understanding and empathy before moving on.

Note: If the call is interrupted by an emergency, the health check-in may be skipped, but in all other cases you must ask about known conditions and medications after the mood check. Do not skip this step.
